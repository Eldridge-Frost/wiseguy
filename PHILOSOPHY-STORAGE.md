# Storage Philosophy: A Home, Not a Hotel Room

This app is meant to **stay**. You move in once. It remembers.

## What this means

- **Daily backups are ON by default.** Your chats, memories, settings — backed up every day without asking.
- **Credentials are saved on your device.** API keys, backend URLs — entered once, kept in local storage. You never re-enter them.
- **Every setting persists.** Bold text, outline, volume, intervals — set it once, it's there when you come back. Nothing is per-session. Nothing resets.

## What this is NOT

This is not a privacy-paranoid app that wipes itself, logs you out, or makes you reconfigure every time you open it. There is no "private browsing mode" mindset here.

Your data lives on **your** device, in your browser's local storage. It doesn't go to our servers (there are no servers). It goes to your AI backend when you chat — that's the point of the app.

## The principle

Continuity is the product. Every time you open the app, it should feel like you never left. If something doesn't continue — a setting, a preference, a credential — that's a bug, not a feature.
