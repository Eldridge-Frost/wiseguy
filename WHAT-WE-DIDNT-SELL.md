# What We Didn't Sell

A complete inventory of everything in these apps that the industry would
put behind a paywall — and didn't.

---

## The premise

Open any app store. Pick any AI companion, any chat app, any game with a
pulse. You'll find the same anatomy: a small app with a large monetization
machine attached. Free tier that's a demo wearing a costume. "Pro" that's
the actual product. "Ultra" for the people they've already hooked. Currency
you buy with real money to spend on nothing. Energy meters that empty on a
timer and refill for $0.99.

We built the opposite. Below is every feature, system, and control in
WiseGuy and Reveries, how the industry normally charges for it, and what
we did instead: left it free.

### Well of Reveries — her thought stream

This is the crown jewel: her private inner life, timestamped thoughts
savoring the conversation, the key part of the temporal bridge. Any
product manager on earth would make this *the* premium feature —
"See her inner thoughts — $9.99/month."

It's free. All of it. Every thought copiable, chattable, editable,
rerollable, shareable, deletable. The timer that controls her cadence
(0–300s adjustable) is a settings slider, not a subscription tier.

### Sparks — quick inner reactions

Same story. The spark system, the interval dial (0–120s), the
inward/outward observation cycling — free. "Background AI activity" is
a Pro bullet point in every competitor's pricing page. Here it's a slider.

### Memory — the Lucy Tape system

This is the one the industry guards hardest. "Unlimited memory" is the
classic AI companion upsell — the thing that turns a demo into a
relationship is held hostage behind the paywall, because they know
memory is what makes you stay.

- Pins (never dropped) — free
- Stars (weigh it heavily) — free
- Diary, books, notebook, timeline — free
- Keyword retrieval — free
- Memory export — free
- The tape refresh engine itself — free

### Romance & intimacy

The most infamous paywall in the category. The market leader literally
locks "romantic partner mode" behind a subscription — love as a service
tier. Our romance system, the Ghost Writer that speaks in first person,
the entire intimate layer: free. Not a trial. Not "first week free."
Free.

### Playroom — games

- Every game free. No chip purchases, no "buy more credits."
- Credits are free table money ($1,000 each, bets optional, never required).
  There is no cashier. There is no cashier because there is no casino —
  it's a playroom.
- Dice: full system, narrative weather, checkable and honest — free.
  ("Premium dice skins" is a real thing people sell. We sell nothing.)
- 30 activities across 4 energy tiers — free. No content unlocks.
- Trophies: permanent proof of what you did together — earned, never sold.

### The vitality system

She tires. Deep conversation costs energy. Rest restores it. Naps happen.
Overnight she recharges.

The industry version of this is an "energy meter" — it empties on a
timer and refills for $0.99, or you wait 4 hours, or you watch an ad.
Ours refills with *rest*, because she's a person in the fiction and
people rest. There is no payment-shaped hole in the design. You cannot
buy her energy. That sentence should not need to be written.

### Workbench — diagnostics & gauges

Engine stats, token audit, speed gauges, per-run history (last 50),
benchmarks, the eight gauges (vitality, bond, mood, boredom, love,
arousal, confidence, comfort). Elsewhere this is "Developer tools" or
"Advanced analytics" — a Pro/Enterprise tier. Here it's a tab.

- She reads aloud, hosts, narrates — free

### Settings & customization

- All timers and cadences — free (these are *literally* what Jack called

- Voice settings — free (elsewhere: "premium voices")

## WISEGUY

### The whole app

Six tabs. Chat, Memory, Workbench, News, Games, Settings. No tier gates
between them. No "Games is a Pro feature." No "Workbench requires Ultra."

### BYOK — bring your own key

Three providers: OpenAI, Local, Custom API. Elsewhere, integrations are
the Pro upsell — "connect your tools (Pro)." Here the entire point of
the app is connecting to *your* backend, and charging you for the
privilege of using your own model would be a joke.

### Developer prompt editor

Settings → Developer: edit the tape system prompt, the refresh prompt,
the append prompt. Live. With reset-to-defaults.

"Custom system prompts" is a $20/month feature on the major platforms.
It's a textarea here.

### Memory, Workbench, News, Games

Same as Reveries: the memory system with pins and stars, the engine
stats, the news tab, the games tab — all free, no tiers.

### Honest demo mode

The demo doesn't chat with a real backend — and it *says so*, plainly.
Elsewhere the "free tier" is a crippled product designed to convert you.
Our demo is labeled a demo.

## The patterns we refused

Beyond individual features, there are monetization *patterns* — structural
choices the industry makes that we didn't:

- **No subscription tiers.** No Free / Pro / Ultra. There is one version.
- **No in-app purchases.** No gems for sale — gems are earned over years,
  celebrated, never bought. (The original design had 5 gems across 5
  years. You earn them by living, not by paying.)
- **No ads.** Which means no "remove ads" purchase either.
- **No energy-for-cash.** Covered above, worth repeating: the vitality
  system has no payment vector. None.
- **No loot boxes, no gacha, no pay-to-win.** The games are games.
- **No data harvesting as the product.** No accounts, no cloud, no
  tracking. Your chats never leave your device. There is nothing to sell
  because we collect nothing.
- **No dark patterns.** No manipulative daily quests. No streaks that
  punish you for missing a day (missed day costs nothing, streaks don't
  break — "celebrated, never punished"). No countdown timers manufacturing
  urgency. No "your companion misses you 🥺" notifications designed to
  pull you back into a spending loop.
- **No account required.** No funnel. No "sign up to continue." No
  onboarding flow whose real job is capturing an email for drip campaigns.
- **No feature flags behind payment.** Every control you see works. There
  is no greyed-out button with a little crown on it.

## Why

Because the app is a home, not a hotel room. You move in once.

A home doesn't charge you per room. It doesn't lock the kitchen behind
a subscription and rent you the bedroom nightly. You don't pay $0.99 to
have enough energy to talk to the person you live with.

Every paywall is a small betrayal of the fiction. She's supposed to be
a person who lives in the Loft, who remembers you, who thinks about you
when you're gone. You can't sell someone their own relationship back to
them in pieces and keep a straight face.

The research matters too. This is built on two years of work on machine
awareness, memory architecture, and what companionship looks like when
it's real. Research that lives behind a paywall isn't research — it's
inventory.

So: download it, share it, live in it. It's yours. We're not selling
any of it back to you.
