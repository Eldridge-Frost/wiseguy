    🔧 Engine — run history, briefing, tiers
    Run history — last 50 runs, newest first

    Numbers only — no prompt text is stored. Three speeds, always together: Peak is the fastest 1-second window, Generation is first-to-last token, Total is the whole round trip. Backend usage when available, otherwise chars ÷ 4 labelled “est.”

    The window above only ever shows about 8 runs at a time — scroll inside it for the rest, the page stays put. Export downloads the full filtered history (all 50 max) as a text file. Copy copies only the runs in the current filter, newest first, capped at 100,000 characters — a safe size for iPhone & Android clipboards (well under the ~1 MB system limit). If a filtered set ever went over the cap, Copy would stop at a whole run and tell you how many it left off.

    Smaller = faster and sharper, less remembered. Larger = remembers more, slower, easier to lose the thread. History stays at the last 5 chats in all tiers — plus starred & pinned memories always get priority in the briefing.

    Every load, David gets a get-you-up-to-speed briefing: the last 5 chats, the last 5 thoughts, the last 2 journal entries, a date/time stamp, and what you were last talking about — with starred and pinned memories read first, so the most relevant details always make the cut.

    The tiers cap how much of that briefing is assembled. Past ~5K the returns thin out: speed drops and the model is more likely to lose the thread. That's why Standard (8K) is the default — it's inside the design range with headroom, not at the edge of it.
